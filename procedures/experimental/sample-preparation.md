# Sample Preparation

**Author:** Lab Team  
**Last Updated:** January 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

This procedure outlines the standard protocol for preparing samples for analysis. It ensures consistency and reproducibility across all experiments in the lab.

## Prerequisites

- Completed lab safety training
- Familiarity with basic lab techniques
- Access to the sample preparation area

## Safety Considerations

- Wear appropriate PPE: lab coat, safety glasses, and gloves
- Work in a fume hood when handling volatile substances
- Dispose of waste according to lab protocols

## Materials and Equipment

- Sterile tubes or containers
- Pipettes and tips
- Labeling materials
- Sample storage containers
- Appropriate buffer solutions

## Procedure

### Step 1: Prepare Workspace

Clean and organize the work area. Ensure all necessary materials are available and within reach.

**Expected outcome:** Clean, organized workspace with all materials ready.

### Step 2: Label Containers

Label all containers with:
- Sample ID
- Date
- Your initials
- Any relevant experimental conditions

**Expected outcome:** Clearly labeled containers ready for sample collection.

### Step 3: Collect and Process Samples

Follow specific protocols for your sample type. Document any deviations from standard procedures.

**Expected outcome:** Samples properly collected and processed according to experimental design.

### Step 4: Storage

Store samples at appropriate temperature and conditions. Record storage location in lab notebook.

**Expected outcome:** Samples stored correctly with location documented.

## Quality Control

- Verify all samples are properly labeled
- Confirm storage conditions are appropriate
- Document all steps in lab notebook

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Sample contamination | Non-sterile technique | Discard sample, restart with sterile technique |
| Insufficient sample volume | Incorrect pipetting | Use calibrated pipettes, practice technique |
