# Equipment Maintenance

**Author:** Lab Team  
**Last Updated:** January 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

This procedure outlines general maintenance guidelines for laboratory equipment to ensure optimal performance, extend equipment life, and maintain safety standards.

## Prerequisites

- Completed equipment-specific training
- Familiarity with equipment operation manuals
- Authorization to perform maintenance tasks

## Safety Considerations

- Disconnect power before performing maintenance
- Wear appropriate PPE
- Follow manufacturer safety guidelines
- Do not attempt repairs beyond your training level
- Report any safety concerns immediately

## General Maintenance Principles

### Daily Maintenance

Tasks to perform each day equipment is used:

- Clean exterior surfaces
- Check for visible damage or wear
- Verify equipment is functioning properly
- Record usage in equipment log
- Report any issues immediately

**Expected outcome:** Equipment clean and operational, issues identified early.

### Weekly Maintenance

Tasks to perform weekly:

- Deep clean equipment and surrounding area
- Check consumables and supplies
- Inspect cables and connections
- Verify calibration status
- Review maintenance log

**Expected outcome:** Equipment well-maintained, supplies restocked.

### Monthly Maintenance

Tasks to perform monthly:

- Detailed inspection of all components
- Clean internal components (if applicable and trained)
- Check and tighten connections
- Update maintenance log
- Review usage patterns and performance

**Expected outcome:** Equipment thoroughly inspected and maintained.

### Annual Maintenance

Tasks to perform annually:

- Professional service and calibration
- Replace worn components
- Update equipment records
- Review and update SOPs
- Plan for equipment replacement if needed

**Expected outcome:** Equipment professionally serviced and documented.

## Equipment-Specific Considerations

Each piece of equipment may have specific maintenance requirements. Always consult:
- Manufacturer's manual
- Equipment-specific SOPs
- Service contract requirements

Create and maintain individual equipment files containing:
- Operating manual
- Maintenance schedule
- Service records
- Contact information for repairs
- Calibration certificates

## Documentation

### Maintenance Log

Record all maintenance activities in the equipment log:
- Date and time
- Person performing maintenance
- Tasks completed
- Any issues observed
- Next scheduled maintenance

### Issue Reporting

When problems are identified:
1. Document the issue in detail
2. Tag equipment "Out of Service" if not safe to use
3. Notify lab supervisor
4. Submit service request if needed
5. Follow up until resolved

## Quality Control

- Maintenance schedules followed consistently
- All activities documented
- Equipment performance monitored
- Preventive maintenance prevents major issues
- Equipment available and functional when needed

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Equipment not turning on | Power issue | Check connections, circuit breaker, outlet |
| Unusual noise | Mechanical wear | Stop use, inspect, contact service |
| Poor performance | Needs cleaning/calibration | Perform maintenance, recalibrate |
| Error messages | Various | Consult manual, contact manufacturer |

## Equipment Emergency Procedures

If equipment malfunction could cause safety hazard:
1. Stop using equipment immediately
2. Disconnect power if safe
3. Clear area if necessary
4. Report to supervisor
5. Tag "DANGER - Do Not Use"
