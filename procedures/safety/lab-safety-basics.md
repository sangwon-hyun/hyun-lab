# Lab Safety Basics

**Author:** Lab Team  
**Last Updated:** January 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

This procedure provides essential safety guidelines for all personnel working in the lab. These guidelines must be followed at all times to ensure a safe working environment.

## Prerequisites

- Completed institutional safety training
- Read and understood lab-specific safety protocols
- Emergency contact information on file

## Safety Considerations

This entire document addresses safety considerations. All points are mandatory.

## General Safety Rules

### Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

Always wear appropriate PPE while in the lab:
- Lab coat (fully buttoned)
- Safety glasses or goggles
- Closed-toe shoes
- Gloves when handling chemicals or biological materials
- Additional PPE as required for specific procedures

**Expected outcome:** Proper PPE worn before entering lab work areas.

### Laboratory Conduct

- No eating, drinking, or applying cosmetics in the lab
- Tie back long hair
- Remove jewelry that could interfere with work or get contaminated
- Keep work areas clean and organized
- Label all containers clearly
- Never work alone with hazardous materials

**Expected outcome:** Safe work practices followed at all times.

### Chemical Safety

- Read Safety Data Sheets (SDS) before using new chemicals
- Store chemicals properly according to compatibility
- Use fume hood for volatile or hazardous chemicals
- Never pipette by mouth
- Clean up spills immediately following proper procedures
- Dispose of chemical waste in designated containers

**Expected outcome:** Chemicals handled safely and properly.

### Biological Safety

- Follow biosafety level requirements for your work
- Use aseptic technique when appropriate
- Decontaminate work surfaces before and after use
- Dispose of biological waste in designated biohazard containers
- Report any exposures immediately

**Expected outcome:** Biological materials handled according to safety protocols.

### Equipment Safety

- Receive training before using equipment
- Inspect equipment before use
- Follow manufacturer guidelines
- Report malfunctions immediately
- Do not modify equipment without authorization

**Expected outcome:** Equipment operated safely and properly.

## Emergency Procedures

### In Case of Emergency

1. **Personal injury or exposure:**
   - Rinse affected area immediately
   - Alert others in the lab
   - Seek medical attention
   - Report incident to supervisor

2. **Chemical spill:**
   - Alert others in area
   - Evacuate if necessary
   - Contain spill if safe to do so
   - Contact appropriate response team
   - Do not attempt cleanup without proper training

3. **Fire:**
   - Activate fire alarm
   - Evacuate building
   - Do not use elevators
   - Meet at designated assembly point
   - Do not re-enter until authorized

4. **Equipment malfunction:**
   - Turn off equipment if safe to do so
   - Alert supervisor
   - Place "Do Not Use" sign on equipment
   - Document the issue
