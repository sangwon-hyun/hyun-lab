While we can definitely add users to your group for you, this is also something
you are empowered to do without needing our assistance. All groups we use on the
Elkhorn and Hummingbird clusters are managed via our CruzID manager system. Here
is the basic process:

Go to https://cruzid.ucsc.edu and log in. Then,

1. Click on 'Advanced' in the menu bar, and navigate to the 'Group Management'
   page.

2. Scroll down through the list of groups you are a member of and click on the
   group you're interested in modifying. Names in blue that you can click are
   groups you are a manager of. Groups in black are ones where you are only a
   member. The group you want in this case is hb-hyunlab.

3. Click the + next to 'Group Members', and then start typing the CruzID of the
   user you want to add in the text box. IMPORTANT: this is an autocompletion
   box - you want to be sure that the person you want to add shows up and then
   click that entry! Just typing their CruzID and hitting 'Add' without allowing
   the autocompletiion to work will result in a failed attempt.

4. You can repeat this process one-at-a-time for each group member. If you had
   many group members to add at once, you can also use the 'Bulk Add'
   option. With the Bulk Add, you will type each CruzID separated by commas. You
   do NOT need to wait for the autocompletiions when adding members this way.

5. If you run into any issues or need additional help getting this working,
   please let me know. We prefer to have our faculty self-manager their groups
   as much as possible as you know when people come and leave, and so you know
   when access needs to be granted or removed, especially if there is an
   imperative nature to the process.
